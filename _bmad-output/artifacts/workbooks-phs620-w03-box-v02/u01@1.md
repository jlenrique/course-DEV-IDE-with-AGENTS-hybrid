## Depth-delta narrative

### Alignment-chain check for one focal objective
Objective: `u03` (section `sec-alignment-chain-check`)
Depth deferred off the slide: A guided workspace where designers trace objective, evidence, practice, feedback, retry, access path, and modality implications for one objective in their own Blueprint.
Give designers a slower companion to the alignment-chain slides so they can test one objective in their own Blueprint without overloading the recording.

### Criteria that two reviewers can interpret consistently
Objective: `u06` (section `sec-criteria-and-evidence`)
Depth deferred off the slide: A criteria-writing worksheet that separates communication indicators from mandatory safety checks and asks designers to identify what cannot be compensated for by other strengths.
Move the exact scoring logic out of narration and into a usable review surface for designers' own lesson criteria.

### Sequence, modality, and planned component register
Objective: `u10` (section `sec-sequence-modality-register`)
Depth deferred off the slide: A planning table where designers describe each phase purpose, mark live or asynchronous with an instructional reason, and draft four planned Panel 5 rows with access commitments.
Provide the read-in-depth partner for the later planning slides so the recording can stay concise while the workbook carries the actual drafting surface.

### One consequential review question and final Blueprint check
Objective: `u17` (section `sec-review-and-submit`)
Depth deferred off the slide: A short checklist for identifying one weak connection, linking observation to consequence and next move, and then checking the cumulative Blueprint before submission.
Close the workbook with a practical ratification surface that mirrors the recording's final Blueprint-readiness check.
