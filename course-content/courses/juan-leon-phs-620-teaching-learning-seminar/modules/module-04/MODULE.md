# Module 04 — Rehearse the Live Instructional Segment

Week 4 of PHS 620. Product 3 opens: the student turns one small part of the
Week 3 lesson plan into a five-minute teaching experience, rehearses it live
with course peers taking the intended learners' roles, and uses one
consequential observation to revise or justify retaining the design.

There is no separate Week 4 upload. Evidence carries into Week 6 Product 3
Completion. Week 5 retests the same five-minute cycle and adds the interactive
prototype.

## Module learning objectives

1. Recognize and address the needs of critical third-party stakeholders,
   including accreditors, administrators, and other reviewers or clients,
   regarding the design and delivery of a learning program.
2. Develop presentations, handouts, videos, interactive activities, or other
   resources that align with the assessments and learning outcomes.
3. Differentiate between different presentation styles — lecture-based,
   interactive, and experiential — and select the appropriate style for
   promoting learner engagement, satisfaction, retention of knowledge, and
   application.
4. Identify the key factors that contribute to learner engagement,
   satisfaction, retention of knowledge, and application in presentations.
